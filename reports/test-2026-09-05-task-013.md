## Implementation

The first checkpoint delivered a strict offline evaluator and synthetic fixtures. Completion adds
the reviewed source sample and corrects two root causes: arbitrary address-like name suffixes were
misread as district/locality, and full address details were moved into nameKey or treated as extra
street/lot numbers. Explicit name-first province-plus-known-Seoul-district boundaries preserve the
entire address key. Recovered address-like names are capped at medium, preserving the original
no-primary safety annotation. Numbered legal localities, building/floor/unit details, parenthesized
parcel numbers and mountain qualifiers are distinguished. Actual road/district/number conflicts
remain vetoes; exact source/display strings, score tiers, Top-3 size and status mapping are unchanged.

The CLI emits deterministic hash/runtime-bound JSON without I/O to providers or output files.
Similar-ID previews are bounded to ten with full count/truncation flags; exact and low-only target
membership are computed on the full result. This avoids oversized opaque diagnostics without
hiding a target miss. The test-only CLI/evaluator and source fixtures are absent from the browser
entry. No dependency, service, public schema/URL, workflow permission or deployment was added.
