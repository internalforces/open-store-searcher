### R1 — Medium, resolved: timeout during mismatch-body cancellation allowed another acquisition

- Location: `src/pipeline/staged-download.ts:157`; regression at
  `src/pipeline/staged-download.test.ts:392`.
- Impact: the first implementation awaited successful cancellation and then emitted retryable
  mismatch evidence even if its internal inactivity/deadline signal had aborted while waiting.
  The collector checked only the caller's abort signal, allowing an extra transaction after this
  internal timeout, contrary to the no-retry-after-abort contract.
- Reproduction: delay the mismatched response's cancellation promise, advance fake time beyond
  the configured inactivity timeout, then resolve cancellation. The targeted regression failed
  against the first implementation because `lengthMismatch` was still present.
- Resolution: the downloader now checks the combined caller/deadline/inactivity signal after
  awaited cancellation and returns an ordinary rejection without retryable evidence on abort.
  The regression passes and asserts no staging files remain. No TypeScript library setting or
  dependency was changed to implement the test.
- Requirement: FR-13 and the explicitly bounded ISS-003 recovery/abort contract.
