## Client repair

`staged-download.ts` keeps the exact range/full equality gate. It additionally rejects malformed,
unsafe-integer or out-of-bounds Content-Length before reading a body. Only a valid in-bounds
pre-body mismatch yields sanitized `expectedBytes`/`declaredBytes` evidence, after successful body
cancellation and a check that the combined caller/deadline/inactivity signal remains un-aborted.

`collect-seoul-archive.ts` allows at most two acquisition transactions: one original and one fresh
limit/range/full sequence. Only the explicit pre-body mismatch can trigger the second transaction.
A repeated mismatch stops; ordinary HTTP/body/size/permission failures and aborts do not retry.
Final probe evidence is used on success, and numeric mismatch history survives recovered success,
probe denial or later inspection rejection. Inspection still removes a rejected staged archive.
The observation CLI now forwards this history instead of reducing it to a generic message.

All streamed byte bounds, exact expected length, hash, ZIP signature/integrity, schema and 195-
category checks remain. No partial file is promoted and no prior accepted data is replaced.
Internal numeric evidence types add no credentials, cookies, business records or public URL change.
The prior no-retry calibration protocol is superseded only by the user's new bounded repair request.
