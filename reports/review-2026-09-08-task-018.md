## Retained product findings and limitations

PRD Section 14.2 search acceptance remains open. In mobile emulation, 1,000-record exact
and address submissions reach 1,433.9 and 1,478.4 ms; at 50,000 records common-name and
no-match submissions reach 2,488.4 and 1,535.5 ms. Diagnostic exact search alone reaches
1,817.6 ms. Desktop 50,000-record common-name submission also exceeds 500 ms (623.4 ms).
Higher-scale exact/address rendering is unavailable above the 1,000-card resource cap.

The 33,861,032-byte 50,000-record JSON is synthetic, not an accepted production artifact.
The 169-second payload estimate is arithmetic under the declared throughput, not an observed
production download. Physical-device performance, actual Pages/CDN behavior, production
distribution, final partition contracts and full-data rendering remain unverified.
Submission timing reaches a paint opportunity, not measured pixel presentation or INP.
LCP uses a finite observation window; five samples do not establish field percentiles.
Diagnostic preparation/search precedes App mounting and may warm code caches, as disclosed.

The performance report and `memory/known-issues.md` preserve the earlier TASK-014 full-data
rendering concern and assign remediation to Performance Engineer / Implementer, with TASK-021
retaining the performance release gate. Accessible rendering changes and public partition
delivery changes keep their applicable design/approval gates. No next task is activated and
TASK-008 remains on hold.
