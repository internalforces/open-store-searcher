### Resolved during review

1. **P1 — Tall candidate focus could hide the candidate identity**
   - Location: `src/app/result-card.tsx:55`; regression at
     `tests/e2e/search.spec.ts:615`.
   - Impact: At a narrow viewport with enlarged text, native Tab focus could scroll a tall
     article to its bottom, leaving its heading outside the viewport. Keyboard users could see
     the focus outline without the candidate name that identifies the focused card.
   - Reproduction: Submit the two-candidate name-only query at 320 CSS pixels / enlarged text,
     move focus to the results, and Tab forward or backward between candidate articles.
   - Resolution: Direct article focus now scrolls the card to the start while child-link focus
     retains its native position. A guarded `event.target === event.currentTarget` prevents
     descendant source/map links from triggering the card scroll. The 320-pixel forward and
     reverse Tab regression checks heading visibility in Chromium, Firefox, WebKit, and mobile
     Chromium. A red failure was recorded before the correction.
   - Requirement: FR-11, baseline FR-16, PRD Section 14.3.

2. **P2 — Browser evidence initially omitted the result-card source link**
   - Location: `tests/fixtures/map-runtime.tsx:11` and
     `tests/e2e/search.spec.ts:714`.
   - Impact: Component tests established that a valid source URL renders as a native link, but
     the initial TASK-017 browser additions used records with `sourceUrl: null`. They therefore
     did not prove the active-task criterion for keyboard navigation across candidate, source,
     and map controls.
   - Reproduction: Inspect the initial source-mode browser fixture and TASK-017 Tab-order test;
     no result-card source link existed in that fixture.
   - Resolution: The source-mode fixture now supplies a fixed HTTPS test source. The four-engine
     browser test verifies forward and reverse focus order, visible source-link focus, zero
     automatic requests, explicit Enter activation, no Referer, and local interception of the
     requested navigation.
   - Requirement: baseline FR-16, PRD Section 14.3.
